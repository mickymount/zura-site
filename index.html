What it does

Turns receipts into clean records

Upload paper or digital receipts, extract vendor, date, totals, and line items, then push reviewed data into export, tax, and accounting workflows.

AI categorization

Auto-tags expenses and keeps uncertain items in review instead of silently posting bad data.

Tax-ready exports

Schedule C-friendly summaries, PDF exports, mileage deductions, and approval-aware reporting.

Audit trail

Immutable receipt history, approval events, export logs, and long-term archive support.

Workflow depth

Works for solo operators now, with team policies, reimbursements, and accounting sync when needed.

Who it is for

Built for real-world operators

  • Freelancers managing deductions, client-billable spend, and quarterly tax prep
  • Small businesses needing approvals, auditability, and cleaner books
  • Teams replacing shoebox receipts, spreadsheets, and manual categorization
